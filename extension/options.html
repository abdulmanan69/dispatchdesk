DispatchDesk Clicker

Connect the extension to your account. Create a token in DispatchDesk under Browser extension.

The token is stored only in this browser. Revoke it any time from the app.

Workspace

Open workspace puts DispatchDesk on the left and Google Voice on the right of the screen. Shortcut: Alt+Shift+D (change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts).

Split

Google Voice account

Which signed-in Google account owns your Voice number. Calls load in your open Voice tab for that account.

How it works

  • Click the toolbar button on any page to list the phone, USDOT and MC numbers it shows.
  • Call goes through the calling provider you picked in DispatchDesk.
  • Add to CRM fetches the carrier from the FMCSA census and saves it to your carrier list.
  • Select text and right-click to call it or look it up. The call panel opens so you can log the outcome in one click.
  • The call panel works your call queue: local time, script, call, outcome keys 1–8, notes, follow-up and email.
  • Calls are never placed for you. Every call starts from your click.
  • Pages are read only when you click the extension. Nothing runs in the background on websites.
